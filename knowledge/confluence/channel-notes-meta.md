# Channel Notes — Meta

Largest channel (~32% of paid spend in Q3 2026).

## Campaigns

| Campaign | Objective | Creatives |
|---|---|---|
| `meta_aeo_purchase_t1` | App event optimisation on purchase, tier-1 markets weighted | META-AEO-01..03 |
| `meta_broad_installs` | Broad targeting, install optimisation | META-BRD-01..03 |

## Notes

- CPI runs ~$1.40 blended; iOS is more expensive than Android, as on every network.
- AEO campaigns need ~50 purchase events per week per ad set to exit learning. Expect noisy
  CPI in the first week after any big change.
- The Ads Manager UI reports in the ad account's time zone (Pacific). Use the warehouse (UTC)
  for reporting.
- Text-heavy creatives can be rejected; resubmission takes 1-2 days (June: META-BRD-03).
- 2026-08: moved the purchase event into the first conversion window for iOS; small
  improvement in D0 revenue per install.
- Meta expanded Android placements in July. CPI and CTR did not change; the late-July Android
  retention drop was traced to the app release, not to Meta traffic.
