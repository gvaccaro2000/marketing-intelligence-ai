# Q3 2026 UA Budget Plan (Jul 1 → Sep 30)

## Summary

| | Amount |
|---|---|
| Total paid UA budget | **$5.10M** |
| Base plan (loaded in networks) | $4.95M (~$53.8k/day, +4% vs June run-rate) |
| Holdback (3%) | $153k, released in September only if guardrails hold |

## Channel split (base plan)

| Channel | Share | Approx. $/day |
|---|---|---|
| Meta | 32% | $17.2k |
| Google | 24% | $12.9k |
| AppLovin | 21% | $11.3k |
| TikTok | 14% | $7.5k |
| Unity | 10% | $5.4k |

Within each channel budgets are split ~50/50 between the two campaigns.

## Country split

US 36%, BR 20%, JP 14%, UK 10%, DE 10%, FR 10%. See *UA Playbook → Market roles*.

## Guardrails

- Blended paid D7 ROAS ≥ 18% on mature D7 cohorts.
- Blended paid D30 ROAS ≥ 42% on mature D30 cohorts.
- Definitions per *Metric Definitions (v3)*.

## Risks noted at approval

- iOS measurement noise on AEO campaigns.
- Creative fatigue on TikTok.
- Android release cadence (two releases planned in Q3).

## Updates

- **2026-07-17:** `unity_video_retarget` held flat (no scaling) because of suspected invalid
  traffic.
- **2026-07-31:** Android retention drop from the 2.14.0 release. No bid changes; Q3 Android
  ROAS forecast lowered by Finance.

- **2026-08-28:** September changes approved: pause `unity_video_retarget` from Sep 1 and move

  ~$54.7k/day, ~2% above base pace.
