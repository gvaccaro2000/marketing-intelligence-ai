How the projection works

Each line keeps its baseline D7 ROAS and CPI at its baseline spend. When spend changes by a factor f, installs and D7 revenue scale by fb, where b = 1 − the diminishing-returns setting. With b below 1, extra spend buys progressively fewer installs, and cuts lose less than proportionally.

Next-$ ROAS is the D7 return on one more dollar at the new spend. Moving money pays off while the line you add to has a higher next-$ ROAS than the line you take from.

Read before acting

This is a planning aid, not a forecast. Past ROAS doesn't guarantee future ROAS: auctions, creatives and seasonality change, and the diminishing-returns curve is an assumption, not a measurement.

Test a shift on a small share of budget first (for example 10% for two weeks) and compare D7 ROAS against the baseline before scaling it. Data is synthetic, for a fictional game.
