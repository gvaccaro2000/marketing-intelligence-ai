# Marketing KPI Glossary

Quick glossary for leadership decks. For the SQL behind each metric see the Analytics space.

| KPI | What it means |
|---|---|
| CPI | Spend divided by installs, paid channels only |
| CTR | Clicks divided by impressions |
| IPM | Installs per 1,000 impressions |
| ROAS D7 | Revenue from the first 7 days of a cohort divided by the spend that acquired it, mature cohorts only |
| ROAS D30 | Same, with 30 days of revenue |
| D1 / D7 / D30 retention | Share of installers still active on day 1 / 7 / 30, mature cohorts only |
| ARPDAU | Revenue divided by daily active users, all users |
| Payer conversion | Paying users divided by DAU |
| Organic | Installs not attributed to any paid network |

## Notes

- Paid KPIs (CPI, CTR, IPM, ROAS) exclude Organic.
- Always state the date range on slides.
- When in doubt, ask in #data-questions.
