# Channel Notes — TikTok

~14% of paid spend. CPI ~$1.10, low CTR (~1%) but solid ROAS.

## Campaigns

| Campaign | Objective | Creatives |
|---|---|---|
| `tiktok_spark_ugc` | Spark Ads using creator posts | TT-UGC-01..03 |
| `tiktok_value_bidding` | Value optimisation with studio creatives | TT-VAL-01..03 |

## Notes

- Spark codes from creators last 60 days; renew before they expire or the ad stops.
- Creatives fatigue fast: CTR typically drops ~20% after 2-3 weeks. Plan a refresh every
  3 weeks (TT-UGC-01 fatigued in late August).
- JP-specific illustrated cuts added to `tiktok_value_bidding` in late July as a separate
  ad group.
